Lumen Notes privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Lumen Notes ("Lumen") is a note-taking and study app for Windows. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • Lumen has no account and no sign-in.
  • Your notes, pictures, files, to-dos, and study history are stored only on your PC.
  • Lumen does not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising IDs, or usage tracking, and it has no ads.
  • Lumen only connects to the internet for features you turn on, and only to the service that feature needs.

What Lumen stores on your PC

Lumen keeps your workspace (notes, pictures, attachments, settings, to-dos, events, flashcard history, and restore points) in your Windows user folder. It never leaves your PC unless you use one of the features below. Uninstalling Lumen and deleting its folder removes it.

Features that use the internet or other devices

Each of these is off until you set it up, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Lumen does not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone.

Children

Lumen is suitable for students. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information at all.
