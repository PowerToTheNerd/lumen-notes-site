Lumen Notes support

Lumen Notes is a note and study app for Windows.

Get help

  • Read the help guide. It covers getting started, every block, flashcards, backups, export and troubleshooting.
  • In the app, press the ? button in the top bar for the same guide.

Report a problem or ask a question

Email support.lumennotes@gmail.com. In the app, Settings › Sync, backup & app › Report a problem opens an email with your Lumen version filled in.

Please tell us:

  1. What you did.
  2. What you expected to happen.
  3. What happened instead.

Do not send your notes unless we ask. Lumen never sends them by itself.
